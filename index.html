Get the app

The recipe registry.

Fond turns any recipe into a precise, cookable program — and keeps its origin, lineage, and creator credit on record. Cook step by step, free. When a dish gets cooked, its creators get the credit.

What the registry does

Capture any recipe

Import from a link, a video, or a screenshot. Fond reads it like a chef and converts it into exact, playable steps.

Cook it step by step

Focused micro-steps with timers and in-line ingredients. Cooking the baseline of any recipe is free.

Provenance on record

Every recipe carries a canonical identity, its lineage, and its attribution — and keeps them wherever it travels.

Credit where it's due

Every cook is recorded against the recipe cooked, so credit accrues to the people who created the dish.

Cook from the record

Capture the recipes you actually cook, play them back step by step, and keep every dish's history — yours and its creator's — on the record.
